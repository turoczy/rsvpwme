# Your calendar feed (paid)

Paid accounts get a private calendar feed of the events they've saved. It puts your events in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar, and keeps them up to date as you Like and mark Going.

## Turn it on

Go to Settings → Calendar & email → **Personal feed subscriptions**.

It's off until you turn it on. And that's on purpose: anyone with this link could quietly keep tabs on where you're going. Click **Turn on calendar feed** to get your private links:

- **Going (ICS)**: add this one to your calendar app.
- **Likes (ICS)**
- **Likes + Going (ICS)**
- **Going (RSS)**, for a feed reader

## Add it to your calendar

Copy the link you want. In Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook, look for the option to add or subscribe to a calendar from a URL, and paste it in.

## Keep it private

The link is the key. Anyone with it can see what you Like and what you're Going to as it changes.

Want to cut someone off? Click **Get a new link**. Calendar apps don't say who's asking, so there's no way to cut off just one person. The old link stops working for everybody. Then re-share the new one with the folks you trust.

You also get a new link automatically when you:

- block someone,
- change your @handle,
- turn the feed off and back on,
- sign out everywhere, or
- switch to Anonymous.

Old links never come back. **Turn calendar feed off** stops it.

## Just one event?

Every upcoming event has an **Add to calendar** button, on free accounts too. It gives you Google, Apple, or a .ics file to download.
