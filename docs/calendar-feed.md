# Your calendar feed (paid)

Paid accounts get a private calendar feed of the events they've saved. It puts your events in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar, and keeps them up to date as you Like and mark Going.

## Turn it on

Go to Settings → Calendar & email → **Calendar feed** and click **Edit**. (The **Calendar feed settings** button in the My events sidebar takes you to the same spot.)

It's off until you turn it on. And that's on purpose: anyone with this link could quietly keep tabs on where you're going. Click **Turn on calendar feed** to get your private links:

- **Going (ICS)**: add this one to your calendar app.
- **Likes (ICS)**
- **Likes + Going (ICS)**
- **Going (RSS)**, for a feed reader

## Add it to your calendar

Copy the link you want. In Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook, look for the option to add or subscribe to a calendar from a URL, and paste it in. A few examples:

- **Google Calendar** (on the web): next to **Other calendars**, click **+**, then **From URL**.
- **Apple Calendar** (Mac): File → **New Calendar Subscription**.
- **Outlook** (on the web): **Add calendar** → **Subscribe from web**.

Most folks want just **Going**. That way the happy hour you Liked on a whim doesn't sit on your calendar looking like a commitment.

Heads up: calendar apps check a subscribed calendar on their own schedule, not ours. Google and Outlook can take up to a day. So if you just said Going and it isn't there yet, give it a while.

## Keep it private

The link is the key. Anyone with it can see what you Like and what you're Going to as it changes.

Want to cut someone off? Click **Get a new link**. Calendar apps don't say who's asking, so there's no way to cut off just one person. The old link stops working for everybody. Then re-share the new one with the folks you trust.

You also get a new link automatically when you:

- block someone,
- change your @handle,
- turn the feed off and back on,
- sign out everywhere, or
- switch to Anonymous.

While you're anonymous, you also get a new link when you remove a follower or a friendship ends (whoever ended it), so a link you shared with that person stops working. Settings tells you when it happens, so you can re-share the new one.

Old links never come back. **Turn calendar feed off** stops it.

## Just one event?

Every upcoming event has an **Add to calendar** button on its page, on free accounts too. It gives you Google, Apple, or **Download .ics** (for Outlook and other calendar apps). That's a one-time copy: if the event changes later, your calendar won't know.
