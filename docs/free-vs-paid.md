## Do I need it?

Probably not to start. Free does the main job: find events, say you're Going, and see which friends will be there.

You'll feel the edges when you want more than that. For example:

- You follow three people and want a fourth. Free stops at 3. (Unfollowing someone frees up a spot.)
- You want the events you're Going to to show up in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar on their own. That's the calendar feed.
- You ask your AI "What am I up to this weekend?" On free, it can search events for you. Your own plans and friends are part of the paid plan.

When you bump into one of those, the site says so and shows you the paid plan right there.

## Buying it

Three ways in. Same plan, same price.

- Go to [rsvpw.me/pricing](https://rsvpw.me/pricing), sign in, and click **Buy**.
- Click the upgrade link wherever the site tells you something is part of a paid account.
- On a free account, if you ask your AI for something paid, its note includes your own link to the paid plan. That link works for 30 minutes and goes straight to checkout, no sign-in. More on [Connect your AI](connect-your-ai.md).

Payments are processed by Stripe, and your receipt comes from Stripe. Sales tax is added at checkout where it applies.

Once you've paid, you land on a page that says you're in. Confirming usually takes a few seconds. If it says it can't find the purchase, give it a minute and reload. Nothing is lost.

## Refunds

Refunds go through Stripe, under its refund policy. A full refund ends the paid plan. If an account is removed for spam, or for breaking the terms some other way, it loses the paid features and isn't refunded. The [Terms](https://rsvpw.me/terms) have the details.
