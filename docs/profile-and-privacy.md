# Your profile and privacy

You pick how visible you are. Change it any time in Settings → Profile → **Profile visibility**.

## The three choices

**Private** (free, and where every new account starts)
Friends only. You pick who sees you and where you're headed. Every follow is a request you approve.

Without an @handle (that's every free account), only your friends (people you follow who follow you back) see your page. With an @handle (paid), the followers you approve see your profile, and everyone else gets your name, photo, @handle and follower counts.

Here's what that looks like. Say you're paid, Private, and you're @yourhandle. A stranger who opens rsvpw.me/@yourhandle sees your name, photo, @handle, how many followers you have and a **Follow** button. That's it. No bio, no events. My friend Stephen, who you approved, sees the whole page.

Some things still show, like your name on invites and events you share. See [What still shows](#what-still-shows) below.

**Anonymous** (free)
Lurk mode. Nobody can find you. Only mutual friends, and people you follow or ask to follow, see your name. Anything you do while anonymous stays hidden. Even if you switch back later.

Your profile reads as "not found" to anyone who isn't a mutual friend. Mutual friends see your name and @handle, but not what you're up to. And anything you Like or mark Going stays hidden. It doesn't even show up in the counts.

Switching to Anonymous also:

- sends followers who aren't mutual friends back to waiting for your approval,
- withdraws the follow requests you've sent,
- turns off your invite link,
- and gives your calendar feed a new address.

One thing it can't do: pull back emails that already went out, or copies people already saved (a synced calendar, say).

**Public** (paid)
A page anyone can see. The events you're going to are public. And search engines can find you here. Unlocks when you upgrade. Your page lives at rsvpw.me/@yourhandle, so pick an @handle in Settings → Profile.

A free account can't pick Public. If it switches back from Anonymous, it comes back as Private.

Already Private and paid, with follow requests waiting? Switching to Public approves them all. You get a "Continue?" check first.

## Your profile details

Settings → Profile has a few optional extras:

- **Display name.** Leave it blank and we use your Google name. Set one and Google's name stops overwriting it when you sign in.
- **Pronouns** show next to your name.
- **Bio** is up to 500 characters.
- **Event sites.** Add your Luma, Eventbrite, Meetup or Partiful handle. They show on your profile, and we use them to smooth the handoff when you register on those sites.
- **Social links.** Just give us your handle. Each one shows as a link on your profile.

## Who can see what I'm going to?

On a free account, your friends can see your name next to events you Like or mark Going. (Remember, you're friends when you mutually follow one another.) Everyone else sees only the counts. Not who you are.

Paid accounts with an @handle are different. If you're Public, anyone can see your name and photo on the event. If you're Private, the followers you've approved can see your name too.

Anonymous? Nobody sees it. Not even friends.

## The one thing anonymous doesn't hide

The people you follow can always see your name. So can people you ask to follow: your name only, no @handle, photo or bio. That's a safety feature. Nobody gets followed by someone they can't identify.

## What still shows

Private and Anonymous close your profile, but some people still see some of it. A few examples:

- Anyone with your invite link sees your name, photo and @handle (if you have one), and so does the preview when the link is pasted into a chat.
- Anyone who opens an event you shared with **Let them follow me** checked (it starts checked) sees your name. Signed out, they also see whether you're going, liked it or are interested.
- People you invite by email see your name in the email.

The complete list is [Who sees what](https://rsvpw.me/privacy#who-sees-what) on the privacy page.

## People search

Search finds accounts with an @handle: paid accounts, plus a few early accounts that got an @handle before there was a paid plan. Signed in or not, anyone can search. If your account is private, people who find you see only your name, @handle and picture. Don't want to be found? Check **Hide me from people search** in Settings → Privacy & people. Followers you've approved can still find you. (No @handle? You won't see the box, because there's nothing to hide.)

Anonymous accounts, and free accounts without an @handle, don't show up when someone else searches by name.

## Your city

Your city is hidden on your profile until you check **Show my city on my public profile** (Settings → Profile → **Default city**). It still picks the events you see either way.

## Blocking

Block someone from their profile, or from your friends list in Settings → Privacy & people. They won't see you, and you won't see them. Any follows between you are removed. They aren't told.

Your blocks are listed in Settings → Privacy & people under **Blocked users**, where you can **Unblock**. Unblocking doesn't bring the old follows back. Either of you would have to follow again.

## Your data

- **Download my data** (Settings → Account) gives you everything we hold about your account as one file.
- **Delete account** is in the Danger zone at the bottom of Settings. You'll type DELETE to confirm. It can't be undone.
- **Introduction attempts** (when you try to [introduce two friends](friends-and-following.md#connect-dots)) are kept for two days, to enforce the daily limit. Then a daily cleanup deletes them.

The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://rsvpw.me/privacy).
