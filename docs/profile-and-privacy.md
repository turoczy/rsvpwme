# Your profile and privacy

You pick how visible you are. Change it any time in Settings → Profile → **Profile visibility**.

## The three choices

**Private** (free, and where every new account starts)
Friends only. You pick who sees you and where you're headed. On a free account, only your friends (people you follow who follow you back) see your page. On a paid account with an @handle, the followers you approve see your profile, and everyone else gets your name, photo and @handle.

Some things still show, like your name on invites and events you share. See [What still shows](#what-still-shows) below.

**Anonymous** (free)
Lurk mode. Nobody can find you. Only mutual friends, and people you follow or ask to follow, see your name. Anything you do while anonymous stays hidden. Even if you go public later.

Your profile reads as "not found" to anyone who isn't a mutual friend. And anything you Like or mark Going stays hidden. It doesn't even show up in the counts.

**Public** (paid)
A page anyone can see. The events you're going to are public. And search engines can find you here. Unlocks when you upgrade.

A free account can't pick Public. If it switches back from Anonymous, it comes back as Private.

## Who can see what I'm going to?

On a free account, your friends can see your name next to events you Like or mark Going. (Remember, you're friends when you mutually follow one another.) Everyone else sees only the counts. Not who you are.

Paid accounts are different. With a public account, anyone can see your name and avatar on the event. With a private one, the followers you've approved can see your name.

## The one thing anonymous doesn't hide

The people you follow can always see your name. So can people you ask to follow: your name only, no @handle, photo or bio. That's a safety feature. Nobody gets followed by someone they can't identify.

## What still shows

Private and Anonymous close your profile, but some people still see some of it. A few examples:

- Anyone with your invite link sees your name, photo and @handle (if you have one), and so does the preview when the link is pasted into a chat.
- Anyone who opens an event you shared with **Let them follow me** checked (it starts checked) sees your name. Signed out, they also see whether you're going, liked it or are interested.
- People you invite by email see your name in the email.

The complete list is [Who sees what](https://rsvpw.me/privacy#who-sees-what) on the privacy page.

## People search

Search finds accounts with an @handle: paid accounts, plus a few early accounts that got an @handle before there was a paid plan. If your account is private, people who find you see only your name, @handle and picture. Don't want to be found? Check **Hide me from people search** in Settings → Privacy & people. Followers you've approved can still find you.

Anonymous accounts, and free accounts without an @handle, don't show up when someone else searches by name.

## Your city

Your city is hidden on your profile until you check **Show my city on my public profile**. It still picks the events you see either way.

## Blocking

Block someone from their profile. They won't see you, and you won't see them. Any follows between you are removed. Your blocks are listed in Settings → Privacy & people, where you can unblock.

## Your data

- **Download my data** (Settings → Account) gives you everything we hold about your account as one file.
- **Delete account** is in the Danger zone at the bottom of Settings. You'll type DELETE to confirm. It can't be undone.

The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://rsvpw.me/privacy).
