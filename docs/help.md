## Quick answers

### Your account

**Where's Settings?**
Click your picture at the top of any page. That opens your profile, with **⚙️ Settings** up top. (No @handle yet? It takes you straight to Settings.) Or go straight to [rsvpw.me/profile/edit](https://rsvpw.me/profile/edit).

**Can I sign in without Google?**
No. You sign in with your Google account.

**Will it ask for my Google password?**
No. RSVP w/ me will never ask for your Google password. Some event links open on other sites, like the page where you register. If one of those asks for your Google password, don't type it in. That page isn't ours.

**How do I sign out everywhere?**
Settings → Account → **Sign out everywhere**. It signs you out on every browser and device, including the one you're on, gives the weekly email a new unsubscribe link, gives your calendar feed a new address, and stops notifications on every device. (If you use the feed, re-add the new address to your calendar app.)

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Settings and use **Delete account**, in the Danger zone at the bottom. You'll type DELETE to confirm. You can delete your account any time, and it can't be undone. Want your data first? **Download my data** is in Settings → Account. If you'd rather we delete it for you, email rick@piepdx.com.

### Email

**How do I stop the weekly email?**
Settings → Calendar & email. Uncheck **Weekly email** and click **Save** in the bar at the bottom. Or use the unsubscribe link in any of them.

**The weekly email isn't showing up.**
Check your spam folder. Then add our sending address to your contacts, so it lands in your inbox. Settings → Calendar & email shows the address, with a link to add it.

### Notifications

**Can RSVP w/ me buzz my phone?**
On a paid account, yes: friend invites and day-before reminders (reminders only on an Anonymous account), not sent between 10pm and 8am your time. Settings → Calendar & email → Notifications, or **Turn on notifications** on your Inbox's Invites tab. On iPhone, sign in in Safari, then add RSVP w/ me to your Home Screen first. See [Notifications](inbox-and-weekly-email.md#notifications).

**A notification button said something I don't understand.**
Every message and what to do about it is [here](inbox-and-weekly-email.md#what-the-buttons-might-say).

**"Send a test" says Sent, but nothing shows up.**
It reached your device. Your device's notification settings are hiding it: check them, plus Focus or Do Not Disturb. The steps are [here](inbox-and-weekly-email.md#the-test-said-sent-but-nothing-showed-up).

### Following and paid

**It won't let me follow anyone else.**
Free accounts follow up to 3 people. Unfollowing someone frees up a spot, and new members who join through your invite link don't use one. The paid plan has no limit. See [Friends and following](friends-and-following.md).

**Why can't I make my profile Public?**
Public is part of the paid plan. Free accounts can be Private or Anonymous. See [Your profile and privacy](profile-and-privacy.md).

**A friend introduced me to someone. Do I have to do anything?**
No. Follow them if you'd like to, or tap **Not now**. Your friend isn't told either way. See [Connect dots](friends-and-following.md#connect-dots).

**How do I stop introductions?**
Settings → Privacy & people → **Introductions**, or **Turn off introductions** on any one in your Inbox. Nobody is told.

**Is the paid plan a subscription?**
No. It's $9.99 + tax, one time. See [Free vs paid](free-vs-paid.md).

**I paid. Where's my receipt?**
Stripe sends it. Search your email for Stripe.

### Your AI

**I paid, but my AI still says "paid plan."**
Start a new chat. That's it.

**I got an email that a new app can see my account, and it wasn't me.**
The email has a link to disconnect it. You can also go to Settings → Integrations → **Connect your AI** and click **Revoke**. More on [Connect your AI](connect-your-ai.md).

### Everything else

**Does it work outside Portland?**
It started with Portland tech, but events can be from anywhere. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#pick-your-place).

**An event is missing.**
Use [Tell us](https://rsvpw.me/tell-us). Paid accounts can also add it themselves, from the event's link. See [Adding events](adding-events.md).
