## Quick answers

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Settings and use **Delete account**, in the Danger zone at the bottom. You'll type a confirmation first. You can delete your account any time. Want your data first? **Download my data** is in Settings → Account. If you'd rather we delete it for you, email rick@piepdx.com.

**How do I stop the weekly email?**
Settings → Calendar & email, or the unsubscribe link in any email.

**How do I sign out everywhere?**
Settings → Account → **Sign out everywhere**. It signs you out on every browser and device, gives the weekly email a new unsubscribe link, and gives your calendar feed a new address.

**Does it work outside Portland?**
It started with Portland tech, but events can be from anywhere. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#pick-your-place).

**Will it ask for my Google password?**
No. RSVP w/ me will never ask for your Google password.
