# Your Inbox, the weekly email and notifications

## The Inbox

Invites, follow requests, and anything else that needs you. Click **Inbox** at the top of the page. A dot next to it means something's new. It has three tabs: All · Invites · Notifications.

- **All**: everything waiting on you (invites, follow requests and introductions).
- **Invites**: everything someone has asked of you, in one list:
  - **Event invites** from friends. Answer **I'm in**, **Maybe** or **Can't**. I'm in marks you Going. This tab also lists the invites you sent, with each friend's answer (or "No answer yet"). Only you see those.
  - **Follow requests**: people who want to follow you. Approve or Decline. Someone who joined through your invite link also gets **Approve and follow back**. See [Friends and following](friends-and-following.md).
  - **Introductions**: a friend thinks you should meet one of their friends. Tap **Follow** to follow them the usual way, or **Not now** to let it go. The friend who introduced you isn't told either way. **Turn off introductions** stops them altogether. See [Connect dots](friends-and-following.md#connect-dots-paid).
- **Notifications**: what happened with your people. For example:
  - "Stephen followed you," with a **Follow back** button. Follow back and you're friends.
  - "You and Stephen are now friends."
  - "Stephen joined through your invite link," with **Review request** if you haven't answered yet.
  - "Stephen used your invite and is going to Tuesday Trivia."

Notifications stick around for 90 days.

Invites from friends also show up on My events, under **Your friends are going**. Your answer goes only to the friend who invited you. An invite disappears once the event is over.

When nothing needs you: "All caught up. Nothing needs you right now."

## The weekly email

A short note on Sunday night: your week ahead (Monday through Sunday), plus what your friends are going to. The subject starts with the week, like "Week of Nov 30, 2026". It holds:

- What's waiting in your Inbox, if anything: a count of follow requests, of followers you haven't followed back yet, and of introductions from friends. Just counts. No names.
- Your friends' picks: things your friends are going to this week that you haven't Liked or said you're Going to yet. On a paid account, also the ones they Liked, and your close friends' picks come first.
- Events you're Going to this week, and events you Liked, with which friends are coming along.

Each section shows up to 3 events, then a link to the rest on the site. Nothing on your list this week? It still comes, and says your week is wide open.

**Turning it off:** it's on by default. Turn it off in Settings → Calendar & email (uncheck **Weekly email**), or with the Unsubscribe link at the bottom of any weekly email. You can turn it back on in Settings.

**Keeping it out of spam:** add the sender address to your contacts. Settings has a "tap to add" link that does it for you.

RSVP w/ me will never ask for your Google password.

## Activity emails

One more email setting: **Activity emails** sends you a note when someone creates an account from your invite, whether that's your invite link or an email invite. Something like "Stephen joined RSVP w/ me from your invite." That's the only one. No emails about events.

It's on by default. Turn it off in Settings → Calendar & email, or with the link at the bottom of the email itself. That link turns off Activity emails only. Your weekly email keeps coming.

## Notifications

On a paid account, RSVP w/ me can buzz your phone or computer. Two kinds, and only the ones you switch on:

- **Friend invites.** When a friend invites you to something. Invites that come in overnight wait until 8am.
- **Day-before reminders.** Around 5pm Pacific the day before anything you're Going to. One buzz, even on a busy day. (Likes don't count.)

We don't send between 10pm and 8am your time (your city's time zone, or Pacific if you haven't picked a city). An invite that lands overnight waits for 8am. A reminder that would land overnight just doesn't go out. A missed reminder beats a 2am one.

Anonymous accounts don't get friend invites, so on an Anonymous account it's reminders only.

### Turning them on

It's two steps, and they're separate on purpose. The kinds are for your account. The device is, well, the device.

1. Go to Settings → Notifications. Check **Friend invites**, **Day-before reminders**, or both.
2. On each phone or computer you want buzzed, tap **Turn on for this device**. Your browser asks if RSVP w/ me can send notifications. Say yes.

(Checked the boxes but forgot to hit **Save**? Turning on the device saves them for you.)

If nothing's checked, Settings says so: "Nothing above is switched on yet, so no notifications will arrive on any device." A device can be on and still get nothing until you check a box.

Then tap **Send a test**. You should get "Testing, testing. Is this thing on?"

**The shortcut:** on the Invites tab of your Inbox, there's a line that says "Want a heads-up when a friend invites you?" Tap **Turn on notifications** and it does both steps at once: turns on this device and checks **Friend invites**. Reminders stay a Settings choice. The × hides the line for good on that browser.

**Turn off for this device** stops them on the one you're holding. Every device you've turned on is listed below that, each with a **Remove** button. And **Sign out everywhere** stops them on all of them.

### On iPhone or iPad

On iPhone or iPad, notifications only work from the Home Screen. (Apple's rule, not ours.)

1. In Safari, sign in first. (Add it while you're signed out and you get a plain shortcut that opens in Safari, where notifications can't work. If that happened, delete the icon and add it again while signed in.)
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. Leave **Open as Web App** switched on.
3. Open RSVP w/ me from the new icon on your Home Screen, not from Safari, and sign in again.
4. Settings → Notifications → **Turn on for this device**, then **Allow**.

Opened from your Home Screen, RSVP w/ me runs without the address bar. That's normal.

On a free account, or signed out, a Home Screen icon is just a shortcut: it opens in Safari, signed in, like any bookmark.

### The test said "Sent," but nothing showed up

Then it reached your device, and your device decided to keep quiet about it. That's a setting on your end, not ours:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings → Notifications → **RSVP w/ me**. Turn on Allow Notifications, plus Lock Screen, Notification Center and Banners. (It only shows up in that list after you've said yes once.)
- **Mac:** System Settings → Notifications → your browser (Chrome, Safari, whichever). Same idea.
- **Windows:** Settings → System → Notifications → your browser.
- **Focus or Do Not Disturb:** if one's on, add RSVP w/ me (or your browser) to its allowed list.
- **Scheduled Summary** (iPhone): it can hold notifications for later instead of showing them now.

Also worth a peek: pull down for Notification Center. The test might already be sitting there.

### What the buttons might say

Turning on a device, the Inbox shortcut and **Send a test** all tell you when something goes sideways:

- **"Sent. Nothing showing up? …"** (Send a test) It went out. See above.
- **"No notifications yet. If no prompt appeared, your browser may be quieting it…"** You closed the browser's prompt, or the browser hid it. Allow notifications for rsvpw.me in its site settings, then try again.
- **"Notifications are blocked for RSVP w/ me in this browser."** (Or the line in Settings that says your browser is blocking them.) You said no at some point. Allow notifications for rsvpw.me in the browser's site settings, then come back.
- **"This browser couldn't set up notifications. Try again in a minute…"** The browser's own push setup failed. Often a blip, so try again. If it keeps happening, check that push notifications are allowed in the browser's settings. Some privacy-focused browsers ship with push switched off.
- **"This browser's notification service isn't one we can send to."** We send through Apple, Google, Mozilla and Microsoft. Some privacy-minded browsers use something else. Try Safari, Chrome, Firefox or Edge, on this device or another.
- **"Too many tries for now. Try again later."** Turning on, testing and removing devices are limited. Give it a while.
- **"Notifications are part of a paid account."** Your plan changed since the page loaded. Reload to see where things stand.
- **"This page went stale. Reload it, sign in if you're asked, and try again."** (In the Inbox: "That didn't work. Reload the page…") You've been signed out, or the page is old.
- **"That didn't go through. Try again in a minute."** Something on our end. Try again shortly.
- **"This device isn't set up for notifications anymore. Turn it on again."** (Send a test) Your browser or phone dropped the connection. Tap **Turn on for this device** again.
