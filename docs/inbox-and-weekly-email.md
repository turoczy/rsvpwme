# Your Inbox and the weekly email

## The Inbox

Invites, follow requests, and anything else that needs you. Click **Inbox** at the top of the page. It has four tabs:

- **All**: everything waiting on you.
- **Follow requests**: people who want to follow you. Approve or Decline. Someone who joined through your invite link also gets **Approve and follow back**. See [Friends and following](friends-and-following.md).
- **Invites**: events your friends invited you to. Answer **I'm in**, **Maybe** or **Can't**. I'm in marks you Going. This tab also lists the invites you sent, with each friend's answer. Only you see those.
- **Notifications**: when someone follows you, you'll hear about it here.

Invites from friends also show up on My events, under **Your friends are going**.

When nothing needs you: "All caught up."

## The weekly email

A short note on Sunday night: your week ahead, plus what your friends are going to. It holds:

- Events you're Going to this week, and events you Liked.
- Your friends' picks: things your friends are going to this week. On a paid account, also the ones they Liked.
- What's waiting in your Inbox, if anything.

**Turning it off:** it's on by default. Turn it off in Settings → Calendar & email, or with the unsubscribe link in any email. You can turn it back on in the same spot.

**Keeping it out of spam:** add the sender address to your contacts. Settings has a "tap to add" link that does it for you.

RSVP w/ me will never ask for your Google password.

## Activity emails

One more email setting: **Activity emails** sends you a note when someone joins RSVP w/ me from your invite. It's on by default. Turn it off in Settings → Calendar & email.
