# Inviting people

Two kinds of invites: your invite link brings someone new to RSVP w/ me, and an event invite asks a friend along to one event. Both are free.

## Your invite link

Find **Invite your friends** on My events or in Settings → Privacy & people. It's tucked away, so click it to open. Click **Create your invite link**, then copy it and send it however you like. A text, a Slack DM, the bottom of an email. Whatever works.

- When someone joins through your link, we let you know so you can make them a friend. If you want. Every join is a follow request you approve.
- On a free account, someone new who joins through your link doesn't count toward the 3 people either of you can follow.
- Your link expires after 30 days. The panel shows the date.
- Up to 20 people can join through it a day. After that it reopens at midnight.
- **Pause** stops joins for now (**Resume** opens it back up). **Get a fresh link** makes a new one, and the old one stops working.
- Everyone who joined through your link shows up under **Friends you brought in**. **Remove** ends that friendship.

**Invite by email:** in the same panel, type up to 10 addresses a day. We email each one your invite link and don't keep the address. We keep only a scrambled fingerprint of it, for 30 days, to enforce the limits. Those 10 a day are shared with event invites by email (below), not 10 each.

Anonymous? Someone who joins through your link sees who you are only if you approve them and follow back. Email invites, and inviting friends to an event, aren't available while you're anonymous, since an invite names who sent it.

Want an email when someone joins from your invite? That's **Activity emails** in Settings → Calendar & email. It's on unless you turn it off.

## Inviting friends to an event

Click **Invite** on an event in the River, or **Invite friends** on the event's own page. You get a few ways to share it:

- **Copy link**, or post it to Bluesky, Twitter, LinkedIn, Threads, Mastodon or Email.
- **Send to my agent** copies the event for your AI. See [Connect your AI](connect-your-ai.md).
- **Invite friends on RSVP w/ me** (click to open it). Type @ and a friend's name. Friends are people you follow who follow you back. Up to 10 friends a day. They'll see it on their page and in their Inbox, and can answer I'm in, Maybe or Can't. **I'm in** marks them Going. Only you see their answers, in your Inbox under **Invites**. On the event's own page, friends who Liked it are listed first, marked "Liked it." Friends who are already Going can't be picked, and ones you already invited are marked "Invited," so nobody gets invited twice.
- **Invite by email** (click to open it). Up to 10 addresses a day. We email each one the event and don't keep the address.

Here's what that looks like. There's a Thursday happy hour you're Going to and you'd rather not walk in alone. Click **Invite**, open **Invite friends on RSVP w/ me**, type @ and start typing Stephen, pick your friend Stephen and click **Send invites**. Stephen sees "(your name) invited you" on his page and in his Inbox. He taps **I'm in**, and now you'll know someone in the room.

**Let them follow me** adds your invite link to what you share. Anyone it's forwarded to sees who you are. Signed out, they also see whether you're going, liked it or are interested. It starts checked. Uncheck it for one share, or turn the default off in Settings → Privacy & people. To stop links you've already shared, pause your invite link or get a fresh one.
