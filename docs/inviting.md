# Inviting people

Two kinds of invites: your invite link brings someone new to RSVP w/ me, and an event invite asks a friend along to one event. Both are free.

## Your invite link

Find **Invite your friends** on My events or in Settings → Privacy & people. Click **Create your invite link**, then copy it and send it however you like.

- When someone joins through your link, we let you know so you can make them a friend. If you want. Every join is a follow request you approve.
- On a free account, someone new who joins through your link doesn't count toward the 3 people either of you can follow.
- Your link expires after 30 days. The panel shows the date.
- Up to 20 people can join through it a day. After that it reopens at midnight.
- **Pause** stops joins for now. **Get a fresh link** makes a new one, and the old one stops working.

**Invite by email:** in the same panel, type up to 10 addresses a day. We email each one your invite link and don't keep the address. We keep only a scrambled fingerprint of it, for 30 days, to enforce the limits.

Anonymous? Someone who joins through your link sees who you are only if you approve them and follow back. Email invites aren't available while you're anonymous, since an invite names who sent it.

Want an email when someone joins from your invite? That's **Activity emails** in Settings → Calendar & email. It's on unless you turn it off.

## Inviting friends to an event

Click **Invite** on any event. You get a few ways to share it:

- **Invite friends on RSVP w/ me.** Type @ and a friend's name. Friends are people you follow who follow you back. Up to 10 friends a day. They'll see it on their page and in their Inbox, and can answer I'm in, Maybe or Can't. You see their answers in your Inbox under **Invites**. Friends who are already Going can't be picked, and ones you already invited are marked.
- **Invite by email.** Up to 10 addresses a day. We email each one the event and don't keep the address.
- **Copy link**, or post it to Bluesky, Twitter, LinkedIn, Threads, Mastodon or Email. On a phone you also get **Text**.
- **Send to my agent** copies the event for your AI. See [Connect your AI](connect-your-ai.md).

**Let them follow me** adds your invite link to what you share. Anyone it's forwarded to sees who you are. Signed out, they also see whether you're going, liked it or are interested. It starts checked. Uncheck it for one share, or turn the default off in Settings → Privacy & people. To stop links you've already shared, pause your invite link or get a fresh one.
