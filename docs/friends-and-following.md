# Friends and following

Follow = one way. Friend = you follow each other and see each other's plans. Close friend = a friend you've starred (paid).

## Following someone

Open their profile and click **Follow**. They'll see your name.

Whether that's a follow or a request depends on them. Following a paid account set to Public? You're following. Done. Everyone else (every free account, plus paid accounts set to Private) gets a request. The button says **Requested** until they approve it. Click it again to cancel.

A couple of things about requests:

- If they decline, nobody tells you. You just can't send them another request for 30 days.
- A request to a free account that nobody answers goes away after 14 days.

## Follow requests

When someone wants to follow you, it lands in your [Inbox](inbox-and-weekly-email.md) under **Follow requests**. You can **Approve** or **Decline**. Declining is quiet. They aren't told.

**Approve** is one way: they follow you, you don't follow them. If they joined through your invite link, you also get **Approve and follow back**, which makes you friends in one click. If that account is brand new, or uses the same name as someone you already know, you get one extra "is it really them?" check first.

Free accounts approve every new follower. So do paid accounts set to Private. A paid Public account doesn't have to: follows come straight in. Except one. Anyone who joins through your invite link always waits in your Inbox, whatever your settings.

## How many people you can follow

**Free:** up to 3 people. Requests you've sent and are still waiting on count too.

But here's the hack. When someone creates a brand-new account through your [invite link](inviting.md) and you click **Approve and follow back**, that friendship doesn't count toward your 3. Or theirs. It takes the follow back, though. A plain **Approve** makes it an ordinary one-way follow, and that uses one of their spots.

An example. You've used all 3. You send your invite link to my friend Stephen. He signs up, you click Approve and follow back. Now you're friends, and you both still have the same open spots you had before.

Need a spot back? Unfollow someone.

**Paid:** follow as many people as you like. If a paid account goes back to free, it keeps the first 3 people it followed. The rest go on hold, and they come back if you upgrade again.

## Finding people

The search box at the top finds people with an @handle, unless they've opted out. That's paid accounts, plus a few early accounts that got an @handle before there was a paid plan. You can always find your own account, and the people you follow once they've approved you, as long as they have an @handle. Anonymous accounts, and free accounts without an @handle, can't be found by name. So the easiest way to bring a friend in is your [invite link](inviting.md).

## Your friends

- **Your Top 8 friends** on My events shows your friends, most active first (the most Likes and Going over the last 90 days).
- **All friends** ([rsvpw.me/me/friends](https://rsvpw.me/me/friends)) lists everyone.
- Click a friend to see what they're going to. That works even when they're on a free account with no @handle: you get a private page for them that only you can open.
- Your friends are also listed in Settings → Privacy & people, each with a **Block**. Block anyone to end it. They won't be told.

## Close friends (paid)

Click **☆ Close friend** on a friend to see the events they're going to first. They lead **Your friends are going** on My events, and they come first in your weekly email's friends' picks. Only you see who you marked. Star them on their profile or on your friends page.

## Paid check

A small check by someone's name means they have a paid account. It supports RSVP w/ me. It's not an identity check.
