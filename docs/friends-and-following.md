# Friends and following

Follow = one way. Friend = you follow each other and see each other's plans. Close friend = a friend you've starred (paid).

## Following someone

Open their profile and click **Follow**. They'll see your name. If their account is private, your follow is a request. It says **Requested** until they approve it.

## Follow requests

When someone wants to follow you, it lands in your [Inbox](inbox-and-weekly-email.md) under **Follow requests**. You can **Approve** or **Decline**. If they joined through your invite link, you also get **Approve and follow back**, which makes you friends.

Free accounts always approve new followers. Every follow waits in your Inbox, including people who join through your invite link. A paid account with a public page can auto-approve followers instead.

## How many people you can follow

**Free:** up to 3 people. Requests you've sent and are still waiting on count too. But here's the hack: folks you invite with your invite link don't count toward the 3 if they're brand new users. That goes for them too: it doesn't use a spot of theirs either.

Need a spot back? Unfollow someone.

**Paid:** follow as many people as you like.

## Finding people

The search box at the top finds people with an @handle, unless they've opted out. That's paid accounts, plus a few early accounts that got an @handle before there was a paid plan. You can always find your own account, and the people you follow once they've approved you, as long as they have an @handle. Anonymous accounts, and free accounts without an @handle, can't be found by name. So the easiest way to bring a friend in is your [invite link](inviting.md).

## Your friends

- **Your Top 8 friends** on My events shows your friends, most active first.
- **All friends** ([rsvpw.me/me/friends](https://rsvpw.me/me/friends)) lists everyone.
- Your friends are also listed in Settings → Privacy & people, each with a **Block**. Block anyone to end it. They won't be told.

## Close friends (paid)

Star a friend to see the events they're going to first. Only you see who you marked. Star them on their profile or on your friends page.

## Paid check

A small check by someone's name means they have a paid account. It supports RSVP w/ me. It's not an identity check.
