# Getting started

RSVP w/ me is one place for the events you care about. And because it's built around the people you know, you can see which friends are going before you walk in the door.

## Where the events come from

Events around here are scattered across a bunch of sites. Luma. Eventbrite. Meetup. Partiful. Calagator. So we pull them into one list. Not everything posted on those sites. Just a set list of calendars and groups we keep an eye on. It started with Portland tech, but events can be from anywhere.

## Sign in

Go to [rsvpw.me](https://rsvpw.me) and sign in with Google. That's the only way in, so there's no password to make. It's free.

The first time, we ask one question: how visible would you like to be? Anonymous, Private or Public. Private is picked for you, and Public is part of a paid account. No wrong answers, and you can switch whenever. [Your profile and privacy](profile-and-privacy.md) explains each one.

The weekly email is on unless you uncheck it. More in [Your Inbox and the weekly email](inbox-and-weekly-email.md).

## Finding your way around

The top of the page has three links once you're signed in:

- **River**: every public event from the calendars we keep an eye on. Soonest first. Tabs narrow it down: Everything, Friends going, Next 7 days, Free and Online.
- **My events**: your own page. Only you can see it. It shows what your friends are going to, what you've picked, and your past events.
- **Inbox**: invites, follow requests, and anything else that needs you.

There's a search box at the top too. Missing an event? [Tell us](https://rsvpw.me/tell-us).

## Like and Going

The heart is **Like**. Like saves an event so you can find it again.

**Going** says you're planning to be there.

Both count, separately. So a Like never gets mistaken for someone planning to show up. So RSVPs are actually truly RSVPs. Not just people being nice.

## Pick your place

Each place has its own page, like [rsvpw.me/l/seattle](https://rsvpw.me/l/seattle). Search for a city or town to find yours.

To make one place your home, go to Settings → Profile → **Default city / region**. The River then opens on your area. Don't see your city? [Pick a place](https://rsvpw.me/l/?pick=1).

If you're signed in and haven't set a city, the place you pick is saved as your city. It stays hidden on your profile until you choose to show it.
